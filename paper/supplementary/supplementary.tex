\maketitle

This document supplements the main text.
Section~\ref{app:proofs} proves the formal results and states the auxiliary lemmas, Section~\ref{sec:ev2gym} documents the modelling in EV2Gym, Section~\ref{sec:training-supp} gives the training details, and Section~\ref{sec:results-supp} collects further results.
Equations, lemmas and figures of the main text are cited with their main-text numbers, and items of this document carry the prefix S.
Abbreviations follow the main text, which studies electric vehicle (EV) charging with vehicle-to-grid (V2G) services and photovoltaic (PV) generation.
The method is the safe attention policy (SA-Attn), trained with proximal policy optimization (PPO) and compared with a multilayer perceptron (MLP), economic model predictive control (MPC) and a heterogeneous graph policy (EV-GNN).
Further abbreviations are the linear program (LP), the Karush--Kuhn--Tucker (KKT) conditions and conformal prediction (CP).
