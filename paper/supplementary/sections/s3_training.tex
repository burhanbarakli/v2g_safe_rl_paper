\subsection{Training loop}\label{app:ppo}

\Alg{alg:ppo} lists the training loop of \method{}, in which the safety layer of \Alg{M-alg:safety} acts as part of the environment.
Each environment writes into its own buffer, and at update time all token tensors are right-padded to the largest $N$ in the batch with masked entries.
Padding changes neither the actor outputs of real tokens nor the value (Lemma~\ref{lem:equivariance}), and the log-probability, entropy and regulariser sums run over masks only.
The learning reward is the profit divided by $N r^{\sm{ref}}$, so that returns of stations with different $N$ have the same scale, and every reported result uses the undivided profit.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{PPO training of \method{} with a variable number of chargers}\label{alg:ppo}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require 16 environments (8 with $N=10$, 8 with $N=20$); actor $\pi_\theta$ and critic $V_\omega$
\While{fewer than $3\times10^{6}$ environment steps}
  \For{128 steps in every environment}
    \State observe tokens, $l$, $u$, $\Ctil$, $\Crev$ and the feasibility flag
    \State sample $a\sim\pi_\theta(\cdot\mid s)$; $x\gets P^{\sm{ref}}a$
    \State $p\gets\Pi_t(x)$ if feasible, else fallback rule \Comment{\Alg{M-alg:safety}}
    \State step the simulator; $r\gets$ \Eq{M-eq:reward}; store $r/(N r^{\sm{ref}})$, $a$, $\log\pi_\theta(a\mid s)$ over tokens with $u_i-l_i>10^{-9}$
  \EndFor
  \State compute advantages with GAE ($\gamma=0.99$, $\lambda_{\sm{GAE}}=0.95$); pad tokens to the largest $N$ in the batch
  \For{10 epochs, minibatches of $B_{\sm{mb}}$ transitions}
    \State clipped surrogate ($\epsilon_{\sm{clip}}=0.2$) on feasible transitions, normalised by $B_{\sm{mb}}$
    \State loss $\gets-$surrogate $+\,c_{\sm{vf}}\cdot$value loss $-\,c_{\sm{ent}}\cdot$entropy $+\,\mathcal L_{\sm{reg}}$ (\Eq{M-eq:reg})
    \State Adam step with gradient-norm clipping 0.5; stop the epochs early if the approximate KL divergence exceeds 0.03
  \EndFor
\EndWhile
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{Hyperparameters and tuning}\label{app:hyper}

\Tab{tab:hyper} lists the hyperparameters of \method{} and of the learned baselines.
Tuning uses the tight 10-charger station and 20 validation episodes that cycle through the three profiles, with seeds disjoint from training and test seeds.
For each of the four learned methods of our own code, a full grid of 18 configurations is trained for $0.5\times10^{6}$ steps with seed 0, and selection uses the training objective of the method.
The conformal variant and the ablations of \method{} inherit the selection of \method{}, the penalty ablations inherit that of Attention+penalty, and EV-GNN keeps its published hyperparameters.
Penalty weights, $\beta$, the architecture and all other PPO constants are fixed a priori and not tuned.

\subsection{Compute budget}\label{app:compute}

The main experiments train five methods with five seeds each, namely MLP+penalty, MLP+safety layer, Attention+penalty, \method{} and \method{}-CP at $\alpha=0.1$.
Four ablations with five seeds and two penalty-weight sensitivities with three seeds add 26 runs, for 51 runs of $3\times10^{6}$ steps, or about $1.5\times10^{8}$ environment steps.
Tuning adds 72 short runs with $3.6\times10^{7}$ steps in total, and the optional EV-GNN baseline and the 5-min sensitivity add 15 and 5 runs.
The conformal variant is evaluated at all four values of $\alpha$ without retraining, because Proposition~\ref{M-prop:conformal} holds for any policy.
