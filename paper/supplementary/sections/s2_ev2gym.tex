\section{EV2Gym modelling notes}\label{sec:ev2gym}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------

This section documents how the model of the main text maps onto EV2Gym at commit \texttt{55ef368c}, and the simulator is used without changes to its source.
All deviations from the defaults of the simulator are made through our own wrapper, configuration files and EV specification file.

\subsection{Charging taper and Assumption A1}\label{app:taper}

In state-of-charge units $\varsigma=E/E^{\sm{cap}}$, let $\delta=\Pnom\Delta t/E^{\sm{cap}}$ be the gain of one full-power step, $\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}\in(0,1]$ the transition state of charge, $m$ the taper multiplier and $\kappa=m\delta/(1-\varsigma^{\sm{tr}})$.
When the EV's maximum alternating-current charging power does not exceed the charger rating, reading the charging routine of EV2Gym at full current gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:taper}
\frac{\varphi(E)}{E^{\sm{cap}}}=\min\{\hat\varphi(\varsigma),\varsigma+\delta\},\qquad
\hat\varphi(\varsigma)=\begin{cases}
\varsigma+\delta, & \varsigma+\delta\le\varsigma^{\sm{tr}},\\
1-(1-\varsigma^{\sm{tr}})\exp\!\Big(-m\dfrac{\varsigma+\delta-\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}}{1-\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}}\Big), & \varsigma<\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}<\varsigma+\delta,\\
1-(1-\varsigma)\,e^{-\kappa}, & \varsigma\ge\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
According to a check script released with the code, the closed form agrees with EV2Gym's charging routine to $1.4\times10^{-14}$ kWh.
The check covers 768 combinations of EV model, efficiency, transition state of charge, multiplier $m\in\{1,5\}$ and step length $\Delta t\in\{5,15\}$ min.

\begin{lemma}[A1 in EV2Gym]\label{lem:taper}
Suppose that the maximum alternating-current charging power of the EV does not exceed the charger rating.
Then, for every step length, the full-power one-step map of EV2Gym in \Eq{eq:taper} is continuous, non-decreasing and 1-Lipschitz.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
At $\varsigma=\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}-\delta$ the first two branches of $\hat\varphi$ both equal $\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}$, and at $\varsigma=\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}$ the last two branches both equal $1-(1-\varsigma^{\sm{tr}})e^{-\kappa}$.
The map is therefore continuous, as a minimum of continuous functions.
Its branch slopes are $1$, $m\exp(\cdot)>0$ and $e^{-\kappa}\in(0,1)$, and $\varsigma+\delta$ has slope 1, so the map is non-decreasing as a minimum of non-decreasing functions, and A1 holds.
Only the middle branch can have a slope above 1, namely when $m>1$.
On the interval $(\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}-\delta,\varsigma^{\sm{tr}})$, the function $\hat h(\varsigma)=\hat\varphi(\varsigma)-\varsigma-\delta$ is strictly concave with $\hat h(\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}-\delta)=0$.
If $\hat h(\varsigma_1)<0$ at some interior point, concavity gives $\hat h'(\varsigma_1)\le \hat h(\varsigma_1)/(\varsigma_1-\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}+\delta)<0$, so $\hat\varphi'(\varsigma_1)<1$.
Wherever the minimum in \Eq{eq:taper} selects $\hat\varphi$ strictly, its slope is thus below 1, and elsewhere the minimum selects $\varsigma+\delta$ with slope 1.
The map is therefore piecewise continuously differentiable with slopes in $[0,1]$ and hence 1-Lipschitz.
\end{proof}

In EV2Gym, $\eta^{\sm c}=1$ and $\varphi(E)=E+\Delta t\,\bar P(E)$, so a 1-Lipschitz $\varphi$ is equivalent to a non-increasing $\bar P$, the extra condition of Lemma~\ref{lem:deficit}.
A taper whose power drops faster than $1/(\eta^{\sm c}\Delta t)$ kW per kWh would violate A1, and certified thresholds would not repair the departure result.

\subsection{Actuation and Assumption A3}\label{app:actuation}

The decision variable $p_i$ is the power that EV2Gym reports, bills and sends to the transformer, and it equals the battery-side energy change per step.
With this convention $\eta_i^{\sm c}=\eta_i^{\sm d}=1$ in \Eq{M-eq:dynamics}, and EV2Gym's charge and discharge efficiencies $\zeta_i^{\sm c}$ and $\zeta_i^{\sm d}$ act only as derating factors on the power limits.

Our specification file fixes one efficiency per EV model, 0.90 for seven models and 0.93 for one, taken from the simulator's table at the model's nominal current.
EV2Gym rounds the normalised action, the command divided by $\zeta_i^{\sm c}P^{\sm{cs}}$ with the charger rating $P^{\sm{cs}}=22.17$ kW, to five decimals and ignores discharge currents below 0.01 A.
It also rounds the stored energy up to 0.01 kWh after every active step without billing the added energy.
The actuation map therefore sends a command as full current when it lies within $\varepsilon_{\sm{sat}}=10^{-3}$ kW of $u_i$, and otherwise rounds it upwards on the five-decimal action grid.
\Alg{alg:safety} lists one complete step of the safety layer with this actuation.
Rounding can break the aggregate limit when $\sum_il_i$ equals $\Ctil$ to machine precision.
The implementation therefore checks the limit after each projection and recomputes a failing row by a bisection that keeps the feasible end of its bracket.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{One step of the safety layer}\label{alg:safety}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require raw action $x\in\R^N$ (non-finite entries set to 0); for each connected EV: $E_i(t)$, $\tau_i(t)$, certified table $\Bh_i$; limits $C(t)$, $\Crev(t)$
\For{each occupied charger $i$}
  \State $u_i\gets\bar P_i(E_i)$;\quad $\pmn_i\gets$ \Eq{M-eq:limits}
  \If{$E_i\ge\Bh_i(\tau_i)-10^{-9}$} $l_i\gets\min\{u_i,\max\{\pmn_i,\,g_i^{-1}((\Bh_i(\tau_i-1)-E_i)/\Delta t)\}\}$ \Else\ $l_i\gets u_i$ \EndIf
  \State $\chi_i\gets(E_i-\Bh_i(\tau_i))/(\eta_i^{\sm c}\Pnom_i\Delta t)$
\EndFor
\State $\Ctil\gets C-n(t)\varepsilon_q$
\If{$\sum_il_i\le\Ctil$ \textbf{and} $\sum_iu_i\ge-\Crev$ \textbf{and} $\Ctil\ge-\Crev$} \Comment{feasible step (A5)}
  \State $p^{0}\gets\clip(x,l,u)$
  \If{$\mathbf 1^\top p^{0}\in[-\Crev,\Ctil]$} $p\gets p^{0}$
  \Else
    \State sort breakpoints $\{x_i-u_i\}\cup\{x_i-l_i\}$; find $\lambda^{*}$ by one pass
    \State $p\gets\clip(x-\lambda^{*},l,u)$; if $\mathbf 1^\top p\notin[-\Crev,\Ctil]$, recompute by safe bisection
  \EndIf
\Else
  \State $p\gets$ fallback rule of \Sec{app:fallback}; log the case
\EndIf
\State send $p$ to the simulator: full current if $p_i\ge u_i-10^{-3}$, else rounded upwards on the action grid
\State \Return $p$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{lemma}[A3 in EV2Gym]\label{lem:a3}
Suppose that the maximum alternating-current charging power of every EV does not exceed the charger rating and that efficiencies are constant over current levels.
Let chargers and EVs be three-phase, with one EV per charger and zero dead-bands for charging.
Assume further that V2G is enabled and that all EVs leave before the episode ends, and let $z_0(m)\in(0,1)$ be the positive root of $1-e^{-mz}=z$ for $m>1$.
If $\kappa\le\kappa^{*}(m)=m\,z_0(m)$ for every EV, the actuation map realises A3 with $\varepsilon_q=0.008$ kW.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
We first show that every sub-maximal charging command $p\in(0,\bar P(E)]$, sent as the pilot $p/(\zeta^{\sm c}P^{\sm{cs}})$ of the normalised action, is delivered exactly.
Let $\hat\delta=p\Delta t/E^{\sm{cap}}$ be its gain, with $\hat\delta\le\hat\delta^{*}=\varphi(E)/E^{\sm{cap}}-\varsigma\le\delta$.
For a reduced pilot, EV2Gym shifts the transition point to $\varsigma_x=1-(\hat\delta/\delta)(1-\varsigma^{\sm{tr}})\ge\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}$, and we write $\Omega=1-\varsigma_x$.
The delivered gain is $\min\{\hat\delta,\hat\varphi_x(\varsigma)-\varsigma\}$, where $\hat\varphi_x$ is the map of \Eq{eq:taper} with $(\varsigma^{\sm{tr}},\delta)$ replaced by $(\varsigma_x,\hat\delta)$, and the exponent $m\hat\delta/\Omega$ of the shifted taper equals $\kappa$.

If $\varsigma+\hat\delta\le\varsigma_x$, the step stays in the constant-current branch and the gain is $\hat\delta$.
For $\varsigma\ge\varsigma_x$, the taper branch gives $\hat\varphi_x(\varsigma)-\varsigma=(1-\varsigma)(1-e^{-\kappa})$.
Since $\varsigma\ge\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}$, \Eq{eq:taper} gives $\hat\delta\le\hat\delta^{*}\le(1-\varsigma)(1-e^{-\kappa})$, and the gain is again $\hat\delta$.

In the remaining case $\varsigma<\varsigma_x<\varsigma+\hat\delta$, let $y=\varsigma+\hat\delta-\varsigma_x>0$ and $\psi=y/\Omega$.
Then $\hat\varphi_x(\varsigma)-\varsigma-\hat\delta=\Omega(1-e^{-m\psi}-\psi)$, a concave function of $\psi$ that vanishes at 0 and at $z_0(m)$, so the gain is $\hat\delta$ whenever $\psi\le z_0(m)$.
Since $\psi=1-(1-\varsigma-\hat\delta)/\Omega$, this condition reads $1-\varsigma-\hat\delta\ge(1-z_0)\,\hat\delta(1-\varsigma^{\sm{tr}})/\delta$.
If $\varsigma<\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}$, then $1-\varsigma-\hat\delta\ge1-\varphi(E)/E^{\sm{cap}}\ge(1-\varsigma^{\sm{tr}})e^{-\kappa}$ by \Eq{eq:taper}, and with $\hat\delta\le\delta$ the condition holds if $e^{-\kappa}\ge1-z_0=e^{-mz_0}$, that is, if $\kappa\le mz_0$.
For $\varsigma\ge\varsigma^{\sm{tr}}$, the bound $\hat\delta\le(1-\varsigma)(1-e^{-\kappa})$ gives $1-\varsigma-\hat\delta\ge(1-\varsigma)e^{-\kappa}$.
The right-hand side of the condition is then at most $(1-z_0)(1-\varsigma)(1-e^{-\kappa})m/\kappa$, so it suffices that $\kappa/(e^{\kappa}-1)\ge m(1-z_0)$.
At $\kappa=mz_0$ the two sides are equal, because $e^{mz_0}=1/(1-z_0)$, and the left side decreases in $\kappa$, so $\kappa\le mz_0$ suffices again.
Discharging has no taper, and EV2Gym delivers a discharge command within $[\pmn(E),0)$ exactly, floored at $E^{\min}$ and capped by the discharge limit.

Next we bound the realised power.
A saturated command delivers $\varphi(E)-E=\Delta t\,u_i$, and $u_i-p_i\le\varepsilon_{\sm{sat}}=10^{-3}$ kW.
Any positive command below $u_i-\varepsilon_{\sm{sat}}$ is rounded upwards by at most $\zeta^{\sm c}P^{\sm{cs}}10^{-5}\le2.22\times10^{-4}$ kW, stays below $u_i$ and is delivered exactly by the first part, and EV2Gym's own rounding leaves a value on the grid unchanged.
Negative commands are rounded towards zero by at most $2.22\times10^{-4}$ kW, and if the dead-band applies, the realised power is 0 with $0-p_i<0.0069+2.22\times10^{-4}$ kW.
Otherwise $\tilde p_i\ge p_i\ge\pmn(E)$, so neither the discharge limit nor the energy floor clips the command.
In all cases $0\le\tilde p_i-p_i\le0.0072<\varepsilon_q$ kW, and empty chargers draw zero power.

Finally, the stored energy is $E(t+1)=\lceil E+\Delta t\,\tilde p_i\rceil_{0.01}\ge E+\Delta t\,\tilde p_i\ge E+\Delta t\,p_i$.
Also $E+\Delta t\,\tilde p_i\le\varphi(E)\le E^{\sm{cap}}$, and since every capacity is a multiple of 0.01 kWh that is exactly representable after rounding, the upward rounding cannot exceed $E^{\sm{cap}}$.
\end{proof}

With $m=5$, the root is $z_0(5)=0.99302$ and the threshold is $\kappa^{*}(5)=4.965$.
In our configuration, $\kappa\le2.391$ at $\Delta t=15$ min and $\kappa\le0.797$ at $\Delta t=5$ min for all eight EV models, so Lemma~\ref{lem:a3} applies.
For $m\le1$ the delivery is not exact near the transition point, and the taper multiplier therefore keeps its default value of 5.
Without the tightening of the forward limit by $n(t)\varepsilon_q$, realised powers would exceed the forward limit by up to about $n(t)\cdot0.007$ kW on tight steps, according to a closed-loop check released with the code.

\subsection{Exogenous headroom (Assumption A6)}\label{app:a6}

EV2Gym scales the peak of the inflexible load to $m_LP_{\sm{tr}}$ with $m_L\sim\mathrm{Normal}(0.4,0.1^2)$ and clips the load at $P_{\sm{tr}}$.
PV generation is $\mathrm{pv}(t)\,m_PP_{\sm{tr}}$ with a normalised profile $\mathrm{pv}(t)\le0.853\cdot1.1<0.94$ and $m_P\sim\mathrm{Normal}(0.4,0.1^2)$, both drawn once per episode.
Hence $C(t)\ge P_{\sm{tr}}(1-m_L)$, and with $P_{\sm{tr}}=\rho_{\sm{tr}}N$ and $\rho_{\sm{tr}}\ge3$ kW, the condition $C(t)\ge N\varepsilon_q$ can fail only if $m_L>1-\varepsilon_q/\rho_{\sm{tr}}\ge0.997$, an event of about six standard deviations.
Likewise $\Crev(t)\ge P_{\sm{tr}}(1-0.94\,m_P)$ is negative only if $m_P>1.06$, an event of more than six standard deviations.
A6 therefore fails with a probability below $2\times10^{-9}$ per episode.
Since A6 is exogenous, any violation would show up as a step of case (i), (ii) or (iv) of the fallback rule, so the fallback counts of the result tables check it on every replay.

\subsection{Scenarios, arrival profiles and observation}\label{app:scenarios}

As seen in \Tab{tab:scenarios}, the grid quantities and the arrival rate scale linearly with the number of chargers $N$, whereas prices do not.
The three arrival profiles of EV2Gym differ in their arrival times, stays and requested energies.

Workplace arrivals concentrate on weekday mornings, public arrivals spread over the day with an afternoon and evening peak, and private arrivals occur in the late afternoon and evening with overnight stays.
EV2Gym does not generate an EV whose departure would fall within four steps of the episode end, so overnight sessions are silently missing when the episode starts too late.
The public and private profiles therefore start at 12:00 instead of 05:00.
On the scenario generator alone, the share of dropped spawn attempts then falls to 6.8\% and 3.7\%, and spawn multipliers per profile equalise the mean charger occupancy at about 0.21.
They also equalise the energy demand per charger to within about 5\%, and these scenario-generator statistics are design checks rather than results \TODO{the authors re-measure and confirm them}.
The episode starts empty, and loads and PV are redrawn for every episode.
Our literature configuration reproduces EV2Gym's 25-charger V2G profit-maximisation case with 100 kW, the workplace profile and no load or PV, using our EV specification file.
