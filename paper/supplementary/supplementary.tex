This document supplements the main text.
Section~\ref{app:proofs} proves the formal results, Section~\ref{sec:ev2gym} documents the modelling in EV2Gym, and Section~\ref{sec:pipeline} gives the request pipeline and the perfect-information optima.
The compared methods, the training, the tuning and the statistics follow in Section~\ref{sec:training-supp}, and Section~\ref{sec:results-supp} collects further results.
Equations, lemmas and figures of the main text are cited with their main-text numbers, and items of this document carry the prefix S.
Abbreviations follow the main text on electric vehicle (EV) charging with vehicle-to-grid (V2G) services and photovoltaic (PV) generation at a charge point operator (CPO) station.
The main method is the safe attention-based controller (\method{}), trained with proximal policy optimization (PPO) and compared with a Deep Sets policy, a multilayer perceptron (MLP), rule-based methods and economic model predictive control (eMPC).
Further abbreviations are the linear program (LP), the Karush--Kuhn--Tucker (KKT) conditions, least-laxity-first (LLF) scheduling and conformal prediction (CP).
