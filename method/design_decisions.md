# Design decisions for Phase 2 (Orchestrator, 2026-10-21)

These answer the questions raised in `ev2gym_notes.md`. They are binding for A2/A3 unless the human author changes them at G2.
EV2Gym is pinned at commit `55ef368c25a58476a9eeaed5111f112a63a38ca8` (v2.0.0 code, 2026-09-15). We do not patch EV2Gym. Every deviation goes through our own wrapper or config.

| # | Question | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| DD1 | Efficiency convention | Use EV2Gym's native accounting: the env-reported power p_i (kW) equals the battery energy change per step, E_i(t+1) = E_i(t) + p_i Δt. η only derates the achievable power. The method and theorem are stated in general form, with a monotone, piecewise-linear energy update E⁺ = E + Δt(η_c p⁺ − p⁻/η_d) and a state-dependent max-power map. The experiments instantiate η_c = η_d = 1 at the terminal, with derated limits. The paper states this convention and its consequence (no round-trip loss in arbitrage) as a limitation. | Keeps results comparable with EV2Gym-based literature (EV-GNN, GNN-DT). The general theorem covers lossy models. |
| DD2 | Requested energy | Use the EV2Gym desired energy, then apply an arrival-time individual-feasibility cap: E_i^req := min(E_i^desired, E_i^arr + R_i(τ_i^arr, E_i^arr)), where R is the maximum energy reachable during the stay. The number and size of capped requests are reported. Main scenarios use the EV2Gym default desired capacity. A sensitivity scenario uses desired SoC 0.8. | Without the cap, some arrivals are individually infeasible and no controller can satisfy them. The cap makes the guarantee statement meaningful and the truncation measurable. |
| DD3 | Charging taper | Keep EV2Gym's taper (realism). The safety layer uses a general "maximum reachable energy" operator R_i(k, E) computed by forward simulation of full-power charging (vectorised, cheap). The lower bound l_i is found by bisection, or in closed form when the taper is inactive. The theorem assumes the one-step max-charging map E ↦ E + Δt·P̄_i(E) is non-decreasing in E (true when Δt·Lip(P̄_i) ≤ 1). This assumption is checked numerically for EV2Gym's taper parameters. The constant-power closed form l_i = max(−P_i^dis, (E_i^req − E_i − (τ_i − 1) P̄_i Δt)/Δt) is the special case. | The bound stays exact with the simulator. The closed form remains as the didactic special case in the paper. |
| DD4 | Current-dependent η tables | Use scalar η (a constant efficiency per EV model) through our own EV-spec JSON derived from `ev_specs_v2g_enabled2024.json`. The tables are documented as a limitation. | Current-dependent tables create gaps in achievable power, so the projected power would not always be realisable. |
| DD5 | Ports | 1 port per charger in all scenarios. Token = charger = at most one EV. | Avoids EV2Gym's non-physical shared-current normalisation. |
| DD6 | Inflexible load / PV in the safety layer | At step t the safety layer uses the realised inflexible load and PV of step t (`tr.inflexible_load[t]`, `tr.solar_power[t]`). This assumes the controller measures them at the start of the step. The policy observation contains only forecasts (our own forecast: true value plus Gaussian noise, documented). | The prompt's C(t) needs the current value. EV2Gym's own forecast_mean semantics look odd (notes §Q6). |
| DD7 | Transformer limits | Forward limit C(t) = P_tr − P_inflex(t) + P_pv(t). Reverse limit C_rev(t) = P_tr + P_inflex(t) − P_pv(t) (the export limit). We report our own violation metric max(0, P − P_tr) + max(0, −P − P_tr) on the net transformer power. | Symmetric thermal limit. EV2Gym's reverse-flow metric is ambiguous (notes §Q7). |
| DD8 | Joint infeasibility (Σ l_i > C(t)) | The transformer limit has priority (hard physical limit). Fallback rule: least-laxity-first (LLF) allocation of the available capacity C(t) − Σ_i max(l_i⁻, …) among EVs. The shortfall becomes unmet departure energy, which is logged. Infeasible steps, fallback activations and shortfall in kWh are counted and reported. | A grid limit must hold. The prompt asks for counting. |
| DD9 | Admission control | On arrival, run a forward feasibility check. Solve a small LP (HiGHS): can all connected EVs (with their capped requests) still be served under the forecast capacity profile? If not, reduce the new EV's request by bisection until the check passes. Report how many requests were reduced. Variant without admission control as ablation. | Reduces future infeasible steps. It cannot remove them entirely because forecasts are imperfect, and the paper says so. |
| DD10 | Perfect-information optimum | Our own LP/MILP oracle in Pyomo or cvxpy with the HiGHS solver (open source). Hard departure constraint with the same capped E^req, hard transformer limits, true future prices, loads and arrivals. Taper is relaxed (power limit = P̄ at low SoC). The oracle profit is therefore an upper bound, and the reported optimality gap is conservative (an over-estimate). EV2Gym's Gurobi oracle is not used (restricted licence, soft departure penalty with a units bug, numpy-2 crash). | Hard-constrained comparison is required by contribution C3. |
| DD11 | Gradient through the projection | Main algorithm: PPO with a Gaussian policy over raw per-charger actions. The projection is applied before execution and treated as part of the environment for the likelihood ratio (log-prob of the raw sample). This choice follows [gros2020safe; markgraf2025safe]. An auxiliary penalty β‖a − Π(a)‖² limits drift of the raw actions. The analytic (sub)gradient of the projection is derived in the paper for completeness and used in an optional SAC/TD3 variant. | Simple, unbiased for stochastic policies, cited. Avoids the zero-gradient problem [kasaura2023benchmarking]. |
| DD12 | Departure-time uncertainty (secondary contribution, D1) | Policy and safety layer may use a split-conformal lower bound d̂_i^lo on the departure step from a simple predictor (e.g. quantile gradient boosting on arrival time, weekday, requested energy, arrival SoC). Calibrate on held-out EV2Gym-generated sessions. Guarantee: P(d_i ≥ d̂_i^lo) ≥ 1 − α (marginal, exchangeability). Combined with per-step feasibility, this gives P(EV i served) ≥ 1 − α. Report shortfall and profit versus α ∈ {0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2}. | Removes the strongest assumption (known departure). It reuses the same layer. |
| DD13 | Time step | Δt = 15 min main (EV2Gym default family), 5 min sensitivity. | Matches the EV2Gym literature. |
| DD14 | Algorithm | PPO (own CleanRL-style implementation in PyTorch). | Own implementation is easier with a variable number of tokens. PPO is stable with masks. |
| DD15 | Learning reward | PPO is trained on the profit divided by $N\,c^{\mathrm{ref}}P^{\mathrm{ref}}\Delta t$; all reported results use the un-normalised profit in €. | Returns become intensive, so one critic serves episodes with different $N$. (Confirmed by the Orchestrator, 2026-10-22.) |
| DD16 | Actuation margin | Forward transformer limit tightened to $\tilde C=C-n(t)\varepsilon_q$ with $\varepsilon_q=0.008$ kW; commands rounded up to EV2Gym's $10^{-5}$ action grid, full current when $p\ge u-10^{-3}$ kW. | Covers `round(action,5)` and the 0.01 A discharge dead-band; without it the transformer guarantee fails by up to $n\cdot0.007$ kW (verified by the reviewer). |
| DD17 | EV2Gym taper multiplier | Keep `transition_soc_multiplier` $=5$. | Exact delivery of sub-maximal commands (A3, Lemma E2) holds for $m>1$ with $\kappa\le\kappa^{*}(m)$ and fails for $m\le1$. |
| DD18 | Lower-bound computation | Per-EV certified threshold tables $\hat B_i(k)$ computed once at arrival (bisection on the closed-form taper map); the DD2 cap uses the same routine. | $O(1)$ per EV per step, robust to numerical error, and A4 holds by construction. |
| DD19 | Oracle | Lexicographic LP: minimise total departure shortfall, then maximise profit; plus a rounding-aware variant $J^{\mathrm{LP,ceil}}$ relaxed by EV2Gym's 0.01 kWh ceil gain. | Gives a bound also in jointly infeasible episodes; the variant is a rigorous upper bound inside EV2Gym. |
| DD20 | Admission control details | Feasibility LP with a linear (inner) taper approximation; only the new EV's request is reduced, never those of EVs already admitted; the reduced request is re-certified with the DD2 routine. | Keeps promises made to admitted EVs; LP-feasible plans are physically realisable; A4 stays certified. |
| DD21 | Actor-loss masking | Tokens with $u_i-l_i\le10^{-9}$ kW and fallback steps are masked out of the actor loss and entropy (kept for the critic), with the fixed minibatch size as normaliser. | Unbiased for the score-function gradient, lower variance (reviewer: variance ×0.19 and ×0.83); the clipped PPO surrogate is biased anyway. |
| DD22 | Penalty baselines | Fixed weights $w_E=10$ €/kWh per kWh of departure shortfall and $w_P=10$ €/kWh per kWh of transformer overload energy (either direction); not tuned. | Above 11× the highest price, so no violation pays; equal tuning budget for all learned methods. |
| DD23 | Conformal calibration | One session per independently seeded episode, chosen uniformly at random; calibration (and predictor training) per test configuration (scenario, $N$, tightness level, $\Delta t$, spawn multiplier, date range); coverage reported as the episode-averaged fraction. | Makes calibration points exchangeable with a uniformly chosen test EV; the session law depends on $N$. |
| DD24 | Scenario scaling and arrival profiles | Transformer $P_{\mathrm{tr}}=\rho_{\mathrm{tr}}N$, $\rho_{\mathrm{tr}}\in\{4,7\}$ kW per charger; inflexible-load and PV multipliers 0.4 (scaled by $P_{\mathrm{tr}}$; PV peak below $P_{\mathrm{tr}}$); three EV2Gym arrival profiles with per-profile start hour and `spawn_multiplier` equalising mean occupancy (about 0.21): workplace 05:00/5/weekdays, public 12:00/4/both, private 12:00/2.5/both; $T=112$ at 15 min with `empty_ports_at_end_of_simulation=True`; training mixes profiles and tightness uniformly per episode with no profile flag in the observation; test grid 6 sizes × 2 tightness × 3 profiles = 36 configurations (100 replays, 50 for $N\ge100$) plus EV2Gym's original 25-charger/100 kW configuration. | Per-charger congestion and energy demand comparable across $N$ and profiles (measured: occupancy 0.208/0.224/0.214, energy 9.5/10.5/9.9 kWh per charger per day); the 12:00 start avoids dropping about 60 % of private sessions at the horizon (3.7 % remain dropped); no flag keeps the policy deployable at stations of unknown profile and makes the profile a zero-shot dimension. |
| DD25 | Admission LP targets (IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES I-3) | Default `safety.admission_existing_targets: lp_reachable`: inside the admission LP only, each connected EV's target is min(E_req, Ψ^LP(E_i) − 10⁻⁶), the maximum energy reachable in the LP's taper model. Admitted requests are still never reduced. | With full-battery targets, the linear inner taper minorant never reaches E_cap, so the faithful LP was always infeasible and admission degenerated to "request := arrival energy". |
| DD26 | Optimality-gap reference (I-4) | Main gap is computed w.r.t. the rounding-aware oracle J_LP,ceil (Prop. O1(b)). The gap w.r.t. J_LP is reported in the supplementary material. | EV2Gym's unbilled 0.01 kWh ceil gain lets feasible controllers exceed J_LP slightly, so J_LP is not an upper bound inside EV2Gym. |
| DD27 | EV-GNN baseline (I-8) | Optional baseline. One EV-GNN model per arrival profile, trained with its own script at N = 20, ρ_tr = 4 kW, 5 seeds. Zero-shot evaluation on all (N, ρ_tr) of that profile. Reported as "trained per profile" with this protocol difference stated. GNN-DT is not included (no public code found); the paper says so. | EV-GNN's training script accepts one scenario config per run. |
| DD28 | Sensitivity scope (I-20) | Sensitivities at N = 20, ρ_tr = 4 kW, all three profiles: desired SoC 0.8 (re-using the main policies) and Δt = 5 min (a separately trained policy, γ = 0.99^{1/3}). Configs: `test_grid_sensitivity.yaml`, `configs/ablations/train_attn_safe_dt5.yaml`. | Keeps the sensitivities affordable. The main grid stays unchanged. |
| DD29 | Manuscript length | Main text target about 8,500 words (≈ 25 double-spaced pages) with at most 7 figures plus tables. Full proofs, detailed EV2Gym notes, extra tables and figures, and the hyperparameter grid go to the supplementary material. The projection-geometry and lower-bound concepts share one two-panel figure. | SAUCIS guidance for experimental papers. |
| DD30 | Guarantee framing (review M2, consistency M13) | State the results in two layers. (i) The transformer limit holds at every step for any raw action, on feasible and fallback steps alike, under A3 and the configuration conditions of Corollary 2 (FB-0/FB-2 cannot occur under DD24 scaling). (ii) The departure requirement is a per-EV statement (Corollary 1): every EV that is never hit by an infeasible step during its stay meets its requirement. Theorem 1 (global, under A5) is kept as the clean special case. Title, abstract and contributions use "feasibility-preserving" / "provable per-step feasibility under stated conditions", never an unconditional "guaranteed". Working title: "Attention-Based Safe Reinforcement Learning with a Feasibility-Preserving Safety Layer for Size-Agnostic V2G Charging Control" (final title: human author). | Calibrated claims. |
| DD31 | Station-level request pipeline for all methods (review M3, consistency M9) | The arrival cap (DD2) and admission control (DD9/DD25) are part of the station operator's request-setting, not of the controller. They are applied identically for every method, including penalty baselines, heuristics, MPC, EV-GNN and the oracle. Shortfall is measured against the request after admission for all methods, and admission reductions are reported separately. `attn_safe_noadm` becomes "all methods without admission control" as a second evaluation pass for SA-Attn and its penalty twin only. | Like-for-like comparison. |
| DD32 | Oracle and gap (review M3, M8) | (a) Tighten the oracle LP with EV2Gym's piecewise-linear taper: p_t ≤ min(P̄, ρ_i(E_cap − E_t)) is linear in (p, E), so the taper is modelled exactly up to the step discretisation. The relaxation argument then covers only the 0.01 kWh ceil (J_LP,ceil). (b) Main comparison metric: **penalised profit** J^pen = profit − w_E·(unserved energy) − w_P·(overload energy), with w_E = w_P = 10 €/kWh (DD22) for every method. The gap reference is the soft-constraint oracle LP that maximises J^pen (same weights, rounding-aware). It upper-bounds J^pen of every method, including those with shortfall. Raw profit, shortfall and overload are still reported separately. The hard lexicographic oracle is kept for the cost-of-the-guarantee analysis. | Valid bound for all methods; exact taper. |
| DD33 | Additional baselines and ablations (review M5, M6) | Add: (1) `attn_proj_nolb`: projection with l_i = p_i^min (box + aggregate only, no departure lower bound); (2) `attn_penalty_safedeploy`: penalty-trained attention policy, safety layer applied at deployment only (no retraining); (3) `attn_lagrangian`: PPO-Lagrangian with the attention policy, constraint costs = shortfall and overload, dual ascent on λ (PID-free, lr_λ tuned in the same grid); (4) `deepsets_safe`: size-agnostic per-token MLP with mean-pooled global context (no attention) + safety layer; (5) `sllf_safe`: smoothed least-laxity-first [chen2022smoothed], price-unaware; (6) `price_llf_safe`: price-threshold heuristic (charge below the episode's running median price, discharge above) through the safety layer; (7) `empc_highs` with a 24 h horizon (long-horizon receding LP) in addition to the 10-step eMPC. The zero-padded MLP stays, described as the fixed-size reference. | Reviewer expectations for safe RL and EV scheduling. |
| DD34 | MPC solver consistency (review M6) | Use the HiGHS eMPC port for all N in the main grid. The Gurobi eMPC is run only on the 25-charger literature configuration as a cross-check. | No solver switch inside the grid. |
| DD35 | GNN-DT and EV-GNN (review M6, consistency) | GNN-DT code is public (github.com/StavrosOrf/DT4EVs, MIT). It needs offline trajectory datasets generated by an optimiser. It is an optional baseline with instructions (trained per profile at N = 20 like EV-GNN, DD27), run if compute allows. The paper states the status accurately. EV-GNN is evaluated in-distribution (its training configuration) and zero-shot, and both are reported. | Accuracy; fairness. |
| DD36 | Zero-shot claims and references (review M4) | Zero-shot refers to station size N (and the tightness level ρ_tr = 3 kW, an out-of-training stress level added to the test grid at N ∈ {20, 200}). Profiles are mixed in training, so no profile transfer is claimed. Add an in-distribution reference `attn_safe_trainN50` (trained at N = 50, 3 seeds, all profiles) evaluated at N ∈ {50, 100, 200}. The transfer gap to this reference is reported. | Accurate claim; reference point. |
| DD37 | Tuning protocol (review M7, consistency B3) | Every learned method (attn_safe, attn_penalty, attn_lagrangian, mlp_safe, mlp_penalty, deepsets_safe) gets the same tuning budget: a grid over lr × minibatch × entropy (+ penalty weight w ∈ {1, 10, 100} for penalty methods; + lr_λ for Lagrangian), trained on the training mixture (as the code does) for 0.5×10⁶ steps, seed 0, selected on a validation set of mixture episodes disjoint from the test replays. The paper text is corrected to match. reproduce.sh must run tuning first or require an explicit `--hparams` file; silent defaults are an error. The default minibatch must be in the grid. | Fairness; code-paper match. |
| DD38 | Statistics (review M7) | Unit of analysis = seed (5 seeds; replay-level values averaged within seed first). Main tests: paired across seeds (Wilcoxon signed-rank needs n ≥ 6 for p < 0.05 two-sided, so with 5 seeds use paired t-tests on seed means plus stratified bootstrap 95% CIs over seeds and replays; Wilcoxon on replay-level differences of seed-averaged policies as secondary). Holm correction across all comparisons reported in one table (family = table). Report IQM and probability of improvement [agarwal2021deep]. | Avoid pseudo-replication. |
| DD39 | Economics sensitivity (review M9) | Add a sensitivity at N = 20, ρ_tr = 4, all profiles: (a) battery throughput (degradation) cost c_thr = 0.05 €/kWh applied in reward and in evaluation for all methods; (b) discharge price factor 0.9. Main results remain EV2Gym-native (DD1), stated as a limitation. | Bounds the arbitrage optimism. |
| DD40 | Manuscript scope (review M10) | Prop. 1(iii) (Jacobian) moves to the supplementary material (not used by PPO). Main-text target remains about 8,500 words (DD29). | Length. |
| DD41 | AI disclosure (consistency B1, review M11) | The AI statement must accurately list all AI contributions (literature search and screening, method derivation and proofs, experiment design, code and analysis pipeline, drafting of all sections, figures). The human author decides the final wording and must substantially revise the text, per the SAUCIS AI policy. | Honesty; journal policy. |
